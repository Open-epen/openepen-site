The Problem

Freelancers lose an average of 14 hours per month chasing unpaid invoices. Between sending reminders, re-sending PDFs, answering "can you resend that?", and tracking down late payments, the administrative burden of getting paid can feel like a second job.

Existing tools are either too complex (designed for agencies), too expensive (subscription stacks that eat into margins), or too bare-bones (spreadsheets that don't scale). PayMePro is built for the solo freelancer who just needs it to work.

Key Features

  • One-click professional invoice generation
  • Automatic payment reminders on your schedule
  • Client portal for viewing and paying invoices
  • Recurring invoice support for retainer clients
  • Real-time payment status tracking
  • Expense logging and profit/loss overview
  • PDF export with your own logo and branding
  • Late fee automation
  • Multi-currency support
  • Tax line-item support (sales tax, VAT, GST)
  • Stripe & PayPal payment integration
  • Client contact book with invoice history

How It Works

1. Create a client. Add your client's name, email, and billing address once. PayMePro remembers everything for next time.

2. Build the invoice. Add line items, set your payment terms, include tax lines if needed, and preview the final PDF — all in under two minutes.

3. Send & track. Send directly from PayMePro. Your client gets a clean email with a link to view and pay online. You see exactly when they open it and when they pay.

4. Get paid. Payments land in your connected Stripe or PayPal account. Overdue invoices trigger automated reminders so you never have to ask twice.
