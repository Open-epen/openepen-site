Estimates only. This calculator is a planning tool, not a guarantee of loan approval or affordability. Always work with a licensed mortgage professional for your actual numbers.

Loan Type

Conventional loan: Requires typically 3–20% down. PMI applies when down payment < 20% and is removed once you hit 80% LTV. Best credit scores get the best rates.
FHA loan: Minimum 3.5% down (580+ credit score). Requires Upfront MIP of 1.75% of the loan amount (usually rolled into the loan) plus Annual MIP of 0.55–0.85% depending on term and LTV. MIP stays for the life of the loan if down payment < 10%.
