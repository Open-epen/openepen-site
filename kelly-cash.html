Dual Strategy Engine

Kelly Cash supports two independent trading strategies optimized for different market conditions and stock universes.

🔼 HOD Breakout Strategy

Universe: Small-cap movers ($1–$20, float < 100M)

Entry Signal: Price breaks above High of Day with volume surge (≥2× relative volume)

Exits: Stop loss (5%), take profit (15%), trailing stop (4%), HOD breakdown (3%), EOD close

Best For: Fast, momentum-driven micro-cap trades during premarket and market open.

📊 VWAP Break Strategy

Universe: Magnificent Seven + MU (large-cap tech leaders)

Entry Signal: Price breaks above VWAP with volume confirmation (≥1.5× relative volume)

Exits: Profit target (10%), stop loss (8%), VWAP touchback, EOD close

Best For: Steady, mean-reversion plays on mega-cap stocks with predictable volume.

Built-In Risk Management

Kelly Cash enforces strict risk controls to protect capital and prevent catastrophic losses:

Position Sizing

Dynamic or fixed position sizing with max portfolio allocation per trade (default 10%). Automatically scales based on relative volume.

Daily Loss Limits

Bot halts if portfolio loses more than daily threshold (default 5%). Prevents compounding losses on bad market days.

Multiple Exit Mechanisms

Every position has redundant exits: profit targets, stop losses, trailing stops, strategy-specific exits, and hard EOD close.

Session Windows

Trading automatically begins at 09:45 ET (avoids pre-market shakeout) and closes all positions by 15:30 ET (no overnight risk).
